Pricing

Free while in beta. Free forever if you host it.

There is no feature held back behind a sales call: every capability on this site is in the open-source code and in every cloud workspace. The plans below exist so you know what will change when the beta ends — and what will not.

Cloud — Free beta

Questions people ask before signing up

What happens to my workspace when the beta ends?

It stays yours. The Free tier keeps its name and its contents; the Team tier is additive. If limits are introduced for new workspaces, existing ones get notice inside the console first.

Is my fleet data visible to other workspaces, or to you?

Other workspaces: no — every fleet table is protected by Postgres row-level security pinned to your site id, for people and for machines alike. The operators of yantrika.ai administer the database and can see rows for support and abuse prevention; nothing is sold, shared or used to train models.

Can I move from cloud to self-hosted later?

Yes. It is the same schema and the same code. Export your rows over the REST API (or ask us for a dump), apply the migrations to your own project with yantraops migrate, and point your bridges at the new URL with a new ingest key.

Do I need a credit card?

No. The console asks for an email and a password, nothing else.
